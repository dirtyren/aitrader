# Gold (GLD) CMF crossover strategy

## H1: Reduce CMF period from 20 to 10 for faster signal on 15Min bars
Rationale: Gold's mean reversion is sharp. A shorter CMF period may capture
accumulation/distribution pivots earlier, improving fill rates.
Risk: More false signals (whipsaws) in low-volume periods.

## H2: Widen threshold from 0.10 to 0.15 for stronger conviction
Rationale: Require more extreme money flow readings to reduce noise.
Risk: Fewer signals; may miss shallower but profitable reversion moves.

## H3: Increase target_R from 1.5 to 2.0
Rationale: If gold trends strongly on CMF signals, the current tight target
may cut winners too short. Backtests on VWAP bands show 2.0R worked on 5Min.
Risk: Lower win rate; more trades hit stop before target.

## H4: Add SLV (silver) as second symbol
Rationale: Silver moves with gold but has higher beta. CMF signals may
be more pronounced and profitable on SLV.
Risk: Silver needs wider stops (0.75x ATR vs 0.5x). May need separate tuning.

## H5: Switch to 5Min timeframe for higher signal frequency
Rationale: VWAP bands backtest showed 242 trades on 5Min vs 27 on 15Min.
More data faster for optimization. Same 0.5x ATR + 2.0R config was viable.
Risk: Higher noise, more whipsaws; may need threshold adjustment.

## H6: Add VWAP confirmation filter
Rationale: Only take CMF signals when price is on the correct side of VWAP
(long below VWAP, short above VWAP). This combines money flow with structural
mean reversion, potentially filtering out false signals.
Risk: Fewer signals; requires additional code changes.
